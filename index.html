Dog Encyclopedia

Each card shows one breed, what it is like to live with, and how much work it needs every day.

Beagle

Size
Small. About 33–40 cm tall and 9–11 kg, so it fits easily in a small home.
Personality
Friendly, curious and cheerful. It follows its nose everywhere and usually gets along well with children and other dogs.
Everyday care
Two walks a day plus time to sniff. Brush once a week. Beagles love food, so measure meals to keep a healthy weight.

A beagle's nose has around 220 million smell receptors. That is why it can follow a scent for hours.

Golden Retriever

Size
Large. About 55–61 cm tall and 25–34 kg, so it needs room to stretch out.
Personality
Gentle, patient and eager to please. A classic family dog that loves carrying things in its mouth and being near people.
Everyday care
One to two hours of exercise daily. Brush two or three times a week, more often when shedding. Check the ears and trim the nails monthly.

Golden retrievers were bred in Scotland to fetch birds from water, so most of them still love swimming.

Poodle

Size
Three sizes: Toy (under 28 cm), Miniature (28–38 cm) and Standard (over 45 cm).
Personality
Clever, lively and quick to learn. Often called one of the smartest breeds, and it enjoys learning new tricks.
Everyday care
Daily walks and play, plus a short training game. The curly coat needs brushing every other day and a professional trim every 4–8 weeks.

The poodle's famous haircut started as practical work clothes: thick fur kept the joints and chest warm in cold water.
